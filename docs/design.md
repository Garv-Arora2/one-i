# ONE I Design Constitution

## Design thesis
ONE I is an investigative editorial publication with a playful, curious visual identity.

It should feel intelligent, curious, editorial, human, slightly mysterious, premium, and approachable.

It should not feel like an AI product, SaaS dashboard, fintech terminal, or generic startup landing page.

## Landing
Landing and homepage are different experiences.

Landing direction:
- playful -> mysterious -> investigative
- editorial typography
- expressive composition
- doodle investigator
- magnifying glass containing an eye
- animation used for storytelling
- strong ONE I branding and statement/quote

Avoid the standard hero -> three cards -> CTA template.

## Homepage
- Large lead event
- Smaller events below
- Editorial rows rather than repeated cards
- No categories in V1
- No personalization
- No search
- Information first

## Event page
Interactive investigation:
Crux -> Context -> Timeline -> How it was covered -> Wording + Numbers -> Public Reaction -> Sources. (Market impact is planned for V2.)

Use progressive disclosure and expandable detail.

## Neutrality
Never use visual design to imply good/bad media.
Avoid red/green credibility coding, bias scores, truth scores, warning badges, or a ONE I verdict.

## Language
Use normal reader-facing language:
- "How it was described"
- "Where this came from"
- "What was covered"
- "What wasn't"
- "Different numbers"

Avoid unnecessary technical terms.

## Typography
Editorial display typography + readable body typography + restrained metadata. Typography and composition are part of the identity.

## Color
Restrained palette, mostly neutral with one distinctive ONE I accent. No AI gradients or neon palette. Color must not encode moral judgments.

## Layout
Use editorial columns, rules, timelines, comparisons, whitespace, and asymmetric composition where useful. Do not force everything into cards or grids.

## Motion
Motion should communicate discovery, hierarchy, and transition. Landing can be more animated than the product. Avoid animation spam.

## Responsive
Mobile is a deliberate composition, not a compressed desktop. Preserve the information hierarchy and make complex comparisons readable.

## Anti-slop
No purple/blue AI gradients, glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards, bento grids by default, decorative blobs, gradient text, excessive shadows, generic dashboard sidebars, futuristic AI aesthetics, or unnecessary visual effects.
